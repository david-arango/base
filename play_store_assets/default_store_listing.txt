<b>Clarity over complexity. A calm, 100% local personal finance app.</b>

Most budgeting apps are built for banks and advertisers—packed with account linking headaches, noisy notifications, cloud tracking, and arbitrary month-end resets.

<b>Base</b> takes a tranquil, privacy-first approach. Engineered from the ground up to keep your financial life clear, distraction-free, and 100% on your device.

━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━
<b>KEY FEATURES</b>
━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━

• <b>Perpetual Rolling Balance</b>
Financial life doesn't reset when the calendar flips to the 1st of the month. Base tracks a continuous, rolling cash flow balance so you always know your true liquidity and available runway across time.

• <b>100% Offline & Completely Private</b>
Your money is your business alone. All data is saved exclusively on your phone using an encrypted, on-device SQLite database (Android Room). No accounts to register, no cloud servers, no third-party analytics SDKs, and zero telemetry.

• <b>Automated Income Engine</b>
Eliminate repetitive manual entry. Set your regular income schedule (weekly, bi-weekly, semi-monthly, or monthly) and recurring salary amounts. Base automatically updates your ledger when payday arrives.

• <b>Tactile Transaction Ledger</b>
Log income and expenses in seconds with a responsive, high-contrast interface. Clean color hierarchy uses soothing forest sage for positive cash flow and warm terracotta for expenses.

• <b>Custom Categories & Modals</b>
Organize spending your way. Customize category names, icons, and baseline allocations tailored to your lifestyle.

• <b>Tranquil Flow Analytics</b>
Gain deep insight into your financial habits without the overwhelm. Visual breakdowns show spending distribution, income vs. expense balance, and net savings trends over time.

• <b>Distraction-Free Experience</b>
No advertisements, no upsells, no push notifications designed to induce anxiety, and no monthly subscription fees.

• <b>Adaptive Material 3 Design</b>
Crafted with modern Jetpack Compose for smooth 120Hz performance, edge-to-edge support, and seamless Light and Dark mode themes that adapt to your system preferences.

━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━
<b>THE BASE PHILOSOPHY</b>
━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━

✔ <b>Zero Bank Connections:</b> No Plaid, no MX, no passwords stored in the cloud.
✔ <b>Zero Data Collection:</b> What happens on your device stays on your device.
✔ <b>Offline First:</b> Fully functional with airplane mode enabled.
✔ <b>Free & Open Source:</b> Complete transparency into how your data is handled.

Take back peace of mind with your money. Download Base today.
