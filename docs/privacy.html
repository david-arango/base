🛡️ Uncompromising Privacy

Privacy Policy

Last updated: October 2026 • Effective Date: October 12, 2026

The Summary (TL;DR)

  • ✓ Zero Data Collection: We do not collect, view, transmit, monetize, or sell your financial data.
  • ✓ 100% Local Storage: Every transaction, account balance, and category stays strictly on your physical Android device in an on-device SQLite database.
  • ✓ No Third-Party Trackers: No analytics SDKs, no behavioral trackers, no advertising frameworks, and no crash reports that phone home.
  • ✓ Complete Control: Deleting the app or clearing its data permanently removes all information from your phone.

1. Information We Do Not Collect

Unlike traditional fintech applications, Base does not operate any backend servers or cloud databases. When you use Base, none of the following information is ever transmitted to us or any third party:

  • Financial Records: Income figures, transaction descriptions, pay dates, amounts, categories, and account balances.
  • Personal Identifiers: Your name, email address, phone number, physical address, or device IP address.
  • Bank Credentials: Base never asks for, stores, or connects to your banking credentials, routing numbers, or payment accounts.
  • Usage Telemetry: Features tapped, app session lengths, interaction heatmaps, or diagnostic profiling.

2. How Your Data Is Stored (On-Device Storage)

All data generated within Base is saved locally on your Android device using Android Room and SQLite database technologies:

  • Your database file resides strictly in your device's private app sandbox. Other applications installed on your phone cannot access this data without your explicit permission or system root access.
  • No cloud synchronization takes place. If your device is offline, in Airplane mode, or completely disconnected from the internet, Base continues to function at 100% capability.

3. Device Permissions

Base follows the principle of minimal privilege. We only request operating system permissions strictly necessary for user-driven features:

  • Notifications (Optional): If you choose to configure payday reminders or scheduled recurring transaction alerts, Android requires notification permissions to display them locally.
  • No Network Permissions Required: Base does not require internet access to track budgets, compute rolling balances, or generate analytics.

4. Third-Party Services & Libraries

Base does not integrate third-party advertising SDKs, behavioral analytics suites (such as Google Analytics or Firebase), or social media tracking pixels.

The application is completely open source. You can inspect the complete dependency tree in our libs.versions.toml file to verify that only official Jetpack/AndroidX components and test frameworks are utilized.

5. Data Retention & Deletion

Because your data is stored solely on your device, you hold unconditional control over its retention:

  • Individual Entries: You can delete any transaction or category at any time inside the app.
  • Complete Wipe: You can permanently wipe all records by navigating to Android Settings > Apps > Base > Storage > Clear Storage, or by uninstalling the application. Once deleted, your data cannot be recovered by us, because we never possessed a copy.

6. Changes to This Privacy Policy

If we ever introduce optional features in future versions (such as manual encrypted local export/backup), this policy will be updated accordingly. All updates will be committed publicly to the GitHub repository changelog with full version history.

7. Contact & Source Code

If you have any questions or wish to audit the codebase, Base is maintained by David Arango as an open-source project.

You can reach out or file an issue via GitHub: github.com/david-arango/base/issues.
