Built around four tranquil pillars

Most finance apps are designed for banks or advertisers. Base is designed for your mental clarity.

🛡️

100% Local & Private

Your financial data belongs only to you. Base stores everything exclusively in an on-device SQLite database via Android Room. Zero servers, zero analytics, zero external API dependencies.

⏳

Perpetual Rolling Balance

Life doesn't reset on the first day of every month. Base calculates continuous, uninterrupted rolling balance so you always know your actual available runway and liquidity.

⚡

Automated Income Engine

Define recurring paydays (weekly, bi-weekly, semi-monthly, monthly). When the date arrives, Base automatically credits your ledger with zero manual data entry.

🌿

Calm Design System

Crafted with high-contrast, accessible typography, tactile paper tones, soothing forest sage for positive cash flow, and warm terracotta for expenses.

Why Base is different

A deliberate departure from noisy fintech apps.

Typical Budgeting Apps

  • ✕ Requires bank account logins & cloud sync
  • ✕ Sells or analyzes your aggregate financial data
  • ✕ Push notifications designed to trigger anxiety
  • ✕ Monthly subscriptions and hidden paywalls
  • ✕ Artificial month-end resets that lose context

The Base Philosophy

  • ✓ 100% Offline & on-device Room/SQLite storage
  • ✓ Zero analytics, zero telemetry, zero accounts
  • ✓ Distraction-free, calm interface without alerts
  • ✓ Free and open-source forever
  • ✓ True perpetual rolling balance across time
